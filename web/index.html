What is the model actually doing?

A classical TF-IDF model learns vocabulary and inverse document frequencies from the synthetic skill, opportunity and pathway catalogue. Cosine similarity retrieves overlapping terms. Evidence flags identify missing samples, not eligibility. Similarities are not probabilities.

Limits & data boundaries

Only selected skill names and simulated evidence IDs reach the local backend. No names, offence types, age, gender, nationality, ethnicity, disability or other sensitive attributes are accepted. No data is saved by the server. Your browser holds the current selections until reset or reload.

These small English-only catalogues are not evidence of fairness, credential recognition, employment outcomes or performance in real communities. Unknown language and uncommon skills can miss opportunities. Ask a human adviser and verify providers independently.
